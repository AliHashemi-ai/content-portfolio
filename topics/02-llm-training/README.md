# 🏋️ LLM Training

Everything about training LLMs: pre-training, fine-tuning, alignment and the infrastructure that makes it possible.

[← Back to all topics](../../README.md)

## Subtopics

- **Pre-training**: objectives, compute-optimal training, training stability
- **Supervised fine-tuning (SFT)**: instruction tuning, chat templates
- **Parameter-efficient fine-tuning**: LoRA, QLoRA, adapters
- **Preference alignment**: RLHF, DPO, reward models
- **Reasoning & RL**: training models to think step by step
- **Distributed training**: data/tensor/pipeline parallelism, mixed precision
- **Distillation**: compressing large models into smaller ones
