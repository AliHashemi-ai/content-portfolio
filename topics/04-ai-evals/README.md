# 📏 AI Evals

How to measure what models and AI products can actually do: benchmarks, LLM-as-a-judge, human evaluation and building evals for real applications.

[← Back to all topics](../../README.md)

## Subtopics

- **Benchmarks**: knowledge, reasoning, coding, and their limitations
- **LLM-as-a-judge**: automated grading, biases and calibration
- **Human evaluation**: preference studies, arenas, annotation design
- **Application evals**: evaluating RAG, agents and product features
- **Eval methodology**: metrics, statistical significance, error analysis
- **Safety & red-teaming evals**: measuring harmful or risky behaviour
