# Reading list

Foundational papers, collected from each topic page.

## 🧠 LLM Fundamentals

- [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) (Vaswani et al., 2017)
- [Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361) (Kaplan et al., 2020)

## 🏋️ LLM Training

- [Training Compute-Optimal LLMs, a.k.a. Chinchilla](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556) (Hoffmann et al., 2022)
- [Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback, a.k.a. InstructGPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155) (Ouyang et al., 2022)
- [LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) (Hu et al., 2021)
- [Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) (Rafailov et al., 2023)

## 🗂️ Data for AI

- [The FineWeb Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17557) (Penedo et al., 2024)

## 📏 AI Evals

- [Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding (MMLU)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03300) (Hendrycks et al., 2020)
- [Holistic Evaluation of Language Models (HELM)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110) (Liang et al., 2022)
- [Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685) (Zheng et al., 2023)
